## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-energy, tactile, and celebratory arcade environment tailored for Kindergarten through Grade 5 players. The visual identity channels the kinetic joy of competitive party titles and gamified learning apps—merging physical toy-box tactility with vibrant digital punch.

### Design Movement: Toyetic Neo-Arcade
- **Squishy & Physical:** Buttons and containers mimic injection-molded plastic toys and rubber arcade switches with thick dimensional lips, crisp bevel cuts, and bouncy press states.
- **Dopamine-Forward Gamification:** Mathematical triumph is represented not through spreadsheets or analytical tables, but through explosive badges, oversized floating stars, juicy progress meters, and dynamic celebratory confetti bursts.
- **Friendly Competitiveness:** Friendly rivalries and multiplayer clashing are made joyful and non-intimidating through rounded geometric forms, warm pastel foundations, and playful candy-coated surfaces.

## Colors

The palette balances a crisp, cloudless sky canvas with high-saturation candy accents. Every interactive tone possesses a dark anchor counterpart to construct 3D extruded button bottoms and stroke boundaries.

### Core Swatches
- **Sky Blue (Primary):** `#00A2FF` — Primary calls to action, trophy pedestals, navigation pills, and header highlights. Underside bevel: `#0072C6`.
- **Candy Yellow (Secondary):** `#FFCA00` — High-tier rewards, multiplier gauges, combo streaks, and floating star tokens. Underside bevel: `#D99B00`.
- **Bouncy Mint Green (Tertiary):** `#00E599` — Success states, correctness indicators, play/ready confirmations, and energy meters. Underside bevel: `#00B377`.
- **Bubblegum Pink (Accent):** `#FF3D8B` — Rival badges, clash timers, booster bursts, and streak shields. Underside bevel: `#C91A65`.
- **Playful Purple (Accent):** `#8C45FF` — Mystery chests, boss battles, level-up banners, and avatar gear. Underside bevel: `#6525CA`.

### Canvas & Surfaces
- **Atmosphere Canvas:** `#F0F7FF` — Cloud-tinted soft blue backdrop providing contrast for floating white cards.
- **Cloud Card Surface:** `#FFFFFF` — Crisp, ultra-clean pillowy platforms that keep colorful numbers legible.
- **Container Outlines:** `#D4E5FA` — Subtle secondary card borders when solid color borders are not applied.
- **Ink Navy (Typography):** `#1E2A4A` — Deep rounded indigo for maximum contrast, warmth, and legibility without the harshness of pure black.
- **Soft Cloud Grey:** `#7E8BA8` — Supporting label metadata, timer ticks, and inactive icon frames.

## Typography

The type scale emphasizes immediate legibility, softness, and instant emotional readability for developing readers:

- **Display & Headings (Rubik):** Utilized with heavy weights (700 to 900). Characters have softened geometric corners and robust stroke consistency, rendering mathematical expressions and celebratory game terms punchy and toy-like.
- **Reading & Mechanics (Nunito Sans):** Utilized for problem prompts, brief instructions, and dialogue ribbons. Nunito Sans provides open counters and curved terminals to prevent eye fatigue while preserving the youthful mood.
- **Digit Treatment:** Mathematical operations, streak numbers, and scores always adopt `Rubik` with zero letter-spacing tracking (`0em`) to ensure aligned stacked arithmetic and distinct recognition of numbers like `1`, `7`, `6`, and `9`.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts prioritize large touch targets, thumb-driven interaction zones, and uncluttered play canvases.

### Layout Rhythm
- **Phone Canvas (Vertical-First):** Uses a fluid 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Interactive game pads (number pads, multiple-choice buttons) pin to the bottom 40% of the screen within comfortable thumb arc reach.
- **Tablet / Landscape Arena:** Shifts to an 8-column layout. Gameplay splits horizontally: the battle arena/character avatars sit left or top, and the math operation deck rests right or bottom.
- **Touch Target Rules:** Interactive targets for early elementary users must maintain a strict minimum dimension of `56px × 56px` (ideal default: `68px` height for choice buttons) to eliminate missed taps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical extrusions and volumetric drop shadows instead of blurred institutional lighting.

### Tactile Extrusion Technique (The "Chunky Button" Shadow)
- **Primary Elevated Elements:** Constructed with a solid 3D bottom bevel using a `box-shadow` of `0px 6px 0px 0px [darker-shade]`, paired with an ambient soft bounce shadow of `0px 12px 24px rgba(30, 42, 74, 0.08)`.
- **Card Platforms:** White floating cards feature an outer solid perimeter stroke (`3px solid #D4E5FA` or themed accent) paired with `0px 8px 0px 0px rgba(0, 162, 255, 0.12)`. This simulates thick, floating foam tiles.
- **Active / Pressed State:** On tap or active press, the element shifts down visually (`transform: translateY(4px)`), reducing the solid bottom shadow to `0px 2px 0px 0px [darker-shade]`. This produces immediate physical mechanical satisfaction.
- **Floating Overlays & Modals:** Modals pop in using a heavy backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) with an atmosphere tint (`rgba(240, 247, 255, 0.75)`), accompanied by a heavy comic-style shadow `0px 16px 0px 0px rgba(30, 42, 74, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The design uses a pill-shaped and bubbly geometry (Level 3):

- **Standard Buttons & Inputs:** Styled as full pill capsules (`border-radius: 9999px`) or ultra-soft rectangles (`border-radius: 1.5rem` to `2rem`).
- **Cards & Arenas:** Main interaction boards feature large rounded corners (`2rem` to `2.5rem`), creating a soft, padded, bubble-gum aesthetic.
- **Badges, Stars & Gem Nodes:** Badges take circular, squircle, or starburst contours with smooth fillet radii on outer points to ensure safety and visual comfort.
- **Border Enclosures:** Major visual containers carry continuous, smooth outlines ranging from `3px` to `5px` thickness, mimicking toy molding seams.

## Components

### Push Buttons (Tactile Toy Buttons)
- **Anatomy:** Full pill shape, solid candy color fill, bold white uppercase label (`label-lg`), and an extruded bottom color band `6px` tall.
- **Variants:**
  - *Ready / Go Action:* Tertiary Mint (`#00E599`) with `#00B377` base.
  - *Clash / Attack:* Bubblegum Pink (`#FF3D8B`) with `#C91A65` base.
  - *Secondary / Shop:* Candy Yellow (`#FFCA00`) with `#D99B00` base and Ink Navy text.
  - *Utility / Back:* Cloud White (`#FFFFFF`) with `#D4E5FA` base and Ink Navy text.
- **Interactions:** Depresses downward `4px` when tapped with an elastic rebound.

### Choice / Math Answer Cards
- **Anatomy:** Large rectangular cards (`border-radius: 1.75rem`), pure white background, thick `4px` perimeter border tinted in Sky Blue (`#00A2FF`). The equation or answer digit sits centered in `headline-lg` Rubik.
- **Selected State:** Border turns to Candy Yellow or Bouncy Mint, accompanied by a pulsing star tick in the upper-right corner.
- **Incorrect Answer State:** Shakes horizontally for 300ms, border flashes Bubblegum Pink, and base bevel reduces without giving a punishing tone.

### Cloud Cards & Game HUD Containers
- **Anatomy:** Floats on `#F0F7FF` with a pure `#FFFFFF` body and a `4px` solid border colored `#D4E5FA`. Contains game prompts, player standings, or mini-leaderboards.
- **Padding:** Generous internal breathing room (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) keeping numbers cleanly isolated.

### Star & Streak Badges (Chips)
- **Anatomy:** Pill-shaped pill tags with vivid background gradients (e.g., Yellow to Orange for win streaks). Features a 3D yellow star icon popping out of the left border by `-8px`, and high-contrast numerical multipliers inside (`x2`, `x5 COMBO!`).

### Avatar Clash Pods
- **Anatomy:** Concentric circular rings with a dynamic border (`4px solid #FFCA00` or `#FF3D8B`). Avatars sit inside vibrant pastel circular plates. A mini 3D level-pill hangs over the bottom center of the circle edge.

### XP & Answer Progress Bars
- **Anatomy:** Ultra-thick rounded pill track (`height: 20px` to `28px`), light sky track background (`#E1EEFF`), filled with high-energy Mint Green or Candy Yellow.
- **Detail:** Includes an animated striped candy-cane gloss pattern overlay and a bouncing star cap at the leading edge of the progress fill.

### Reward Modals & Victory Sheets
- **Anatomy:** Puffy white container emerging from the center with an oversized ribbon header. Confetti and particle stars burst from the card perimeters. Buttons stack vertically with oversized tap zones (`64px` height).
